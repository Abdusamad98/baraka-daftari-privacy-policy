## English

**Effective date:** September 17, 2026

**Controller:** Abdusamad Abdukarimov, contact: mobilchi4862@gmail.com

"Baraka daftari" (the "App") is a personal income and expense ledger. This policy explains what data the
App collects, why, where it is stored and what control you have over it.

### 1. Data we collect

**1.1. Account data.** You must sign in with Google or Apple to use the App. The provider gives us your
name, email address, a profile photo URL (Google) and a unique user identifier issued by Firebase (uid).
With Sign in with Apple you may hide your email; we then only receive Apple's relay address. We never
see your password.

**1.2. Financial records you enter.** Only what you type in yourself:

- income and expense entries: amount, currency (UZS, USD, RUB), the amount in so'm and the exchange rate
  used, date, category, necessity level, payment method (cash / card / transfer), comment;
- savings pots (name, purpose, target amount and date, balance) and deposits / withdrawals
  (counterparty, note);
- monthly totals (computed automatically from your entries);
- settings: default currency, theme, month start day, salary day, reminder time, budget limits, manually
  entered exchange rates;
- categories you create (name, icon, level).

The App does not connect to your bank accounts, cards or payment services; it only stores what you
record manually.

**1.3. Receipt photos (optional).** If you attach a receipt photo to an entry, the photo is compressed on
your phone to a JPEG under 1 MB, its EXIF metadata (including location and device details) is removed,
and it is uploaded to Firebase Cloud Storage. The camera and photo library are only accessed when you
choose to add a photo.

**1.4. Exchange rates.** The App downloads daily rates from the public website of the Central Bank of
Uzbekistan (`cbu.uz`). No personal data is sent with that request (as with any internet request, the
bank's server can see your IP address).

**1.5. Data kept on your device.** The following stays on your phone and is not sent to us: cached
exchange rates and when they were fetched, your crash-report preference, scheduled local reminders
(notifications are generated on the device, not by a server) and Firebase's offline cache (so the App
works without a connection).

**1.6. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics).** If the App crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics
may send: device model, operating system version, App version, the technical error description (stack
trace), the time of the error and your Firebase uid (a pseudonymous identifier). Reports **do not
include** amounts, comments, category names, your name or email. Reports are only sent from release
builds, and you can turn them off at any time in **Profile → "Xatolik hisobotlarini yuborish"** (send
error reports).

### 2. How we use data

- to identify your account and show your data only to you;
- to store your records, sync them across your devices and compute reports, budgets and savings figures;
- to show the reminders you enable;
- to improve stability (crash reports only).

We **do not sell** your data, do not show ads, do not share data with advertising or tracking services,
and do not use usage-analytics services — the App does not track which screens you open or what you
tap. No automated decisions are made about you.

### 3. Where data is stored and who processes it

Data is stored and processed by Firebase services provided by Google LLC:

| Service | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Firebase Authentication | Sign in with Google / Apple |
| Cloud Firestore | entries, savings pots, settings, categories, monthly totals |
| Cloud Storage for Firebase | receipt photos |
| Firebase Crashlytics | crash reports |

Firestore and Storage data are stored in the location selected for the Firebase project: **Qatar, Doha (Google Cloud `me-central1`)**.
Google processes the data under its own security and privacy terms
(<https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy>). Sign-in providers (Google, Apple) are governed by their
own privacy policies.

We do not share your data with anyone else, except where required by law.

### 4. Security

- All communication between the App and the servers is encrypted (HTTPS/TLS).
- Firestore and Storage security rules only allow **the owner** (the signed-in account) to read or write
  each document and file; other users cannot access your data.
- Receipt photos can only be stored in your own folder, only as JPEG and under 1 MB.

No system is perfectly secure; if you find a security issue, please write to mobilchi4862@gmail.com.

### 5. Retention and deletion

- Entries, savings pots, categories, settings and receipt photos are kept until you delete them or delete
  your account. Deleting an entry or removing its receipt also deletes the photo from the server; any
  photo file left behind in rare cases is always removed when the account is deleted.
- **Account deletion:** Profile → "Hisobni o'chirish" (delete account). The App asks you to sign in
  again, then deletes all your Firestore data, your receipt photos and your Firebase account. This cannot
  be undone.
- Crash reports are kept for Firebase Crashlytics' standard retention period (currently 90 days) and then
  deleted automatically.
- Data on your device is removed when you uninstall the App.

### 6. Your rights

- **Access and portability:** view all your records in the App and export them as a file via Profile →
  "Ma'lumotlarni eksport qilish (CSV)" (export data).
- **Correction:** edit any entry, savings pot, category or setting in the App.
- **Deletion:** delete individual entries or your whole account in the App.
- **Withdrawing consent:** turn off crash reports, reminders, camera and photo access at any time in the
  App or in your phone settings.
- For any other request, write to mobilchi4862@gmail.com.

We aim to process personal data in line with the personal data legislation of the Republic of Uzbekistan
and the requirements that apply in your country of residence.
